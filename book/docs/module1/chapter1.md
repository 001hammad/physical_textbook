# Chapter 1: Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the very first chapter of this course!  
In this module, we will understand what **Physical AI** and **Humanoid Robotics** really mean, and why they are the future of AI.

### What is Physical AI?

**Physical AI** means AI that can live and work in the real physical world — not just on a computer screen.  

Normal AI (like ChatGPT) works only with text, images, or videos.  
But **Physical AI** can:
- See the real world using cameras and sensors  
- Move things using arms and legs  
- Feel gravity, touch, and collisions  
- Understand and follow physical laws (like "things fall down")

Physical AI is also called **Embodied AI** because the AI is "embodied" (inside a body) that interacts with the real world.

### What are Humanoid Robots?

A **humanoid robot** is a robot that looks and moves like a human.  
Examples:  
- Two legs to walk  
- Two arms and hands to pick things  
- A head with cameras (like eyes) and microphones (like ears)

Why do we make robots that look like humans?  
Because our world is built for humans:  
- Doors, stairs, chairs, cups — everything is made for human size and shape  
- If a robot looks like us, it can use the same tools and spaces easily

### Why Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics Matter

In the future, humanoid robots will:
- Help in homes (cleaning, cooking, helping elderly)  
- Work in factories (lifting heavy things)  
- Explore dangerous places (space, disaster zones)  
- Work with humans as assistants or friends

This is the next big step after smartphones and computers — AI that can walk, talk, and work beside us.
